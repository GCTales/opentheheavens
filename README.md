# Open The Heavens  
This is a work in progress mod Risk of Rain 2 aimed at balancing the rougher edges of the base game and all DLCs.
When complete, it will feature significant changes to the stage loop and alternate paths (such as Conduit Canyon), reworks to certain core systems (such as Hexes or the N'Tormat), as well as various tweaks to items (such as Sonorous Whispers or Leeching Seed), survivors (such as Drifter or REX) and enemies (such as Lesser Wisps or the Solus family).  
  
A handful of these changes will be controversial, or you might simply not like them. I am committed to ensuring that **every rework and change** is configurable.
This mod is not necessarily intended to "fix" Risk of Rain 2, only to provide a jumping off point for further iteration the development is so clearly starved of.  
  
# Compatibility
For a handful of reasons, I cannot guarantee compatibility with every mod. However, there are a few that I want to ensure work as intended:
### Full Compatibility
- **Quality:** I aim for all item reworks I make to be reflected in their quality counterparts.
- **Retroactive Macro:** I see this mod as having a similar philosophy to some of my own planned changes, and if you are interested in the changes Open The Heavens makes, you'll probably be interested in this too.
- **Umbral Mithrix / Fathomless Voidling / Artifact of the King / etc:** There are various mods that rework bossfights. If someone wishes to replace changes I make with them, they should be able to do so without having to faff about.

### Partial Compatibility
- **Starstorm 2 / Sandswept:** While I personally have not tried this mods and am not inclined to at this time, they are well regarded content mods that many may use. I want to ensure that these mods can run side-by-side with Open The Heavens, however I will not attempt to balance them in line with it, as it is not my place to do so. Features in these mods may therefore stick out where their developers' and my own design philosophies differ.
- **Stage Addition / Variation mods:** While generally I feel as though these should not conflict with my changes, it will be hard to ensure every one will work as intended without special considerations.
- **RiskySotS:** This mod has served as an inspiration for changes I plan to make to the Halcyon Shrine, and as such some of its changes will not be able to work while some of mine are active. Configuring Open the Heavens should allow you to choose between those reworks if you prefer one over the other.

### No Compatibility
- **Any mod generated by AI:** These mods are nasty to work with as they may recieve breaking updates at random, not be maintained, or just not function correctly at all. I will not be attempting to make any of the features in Open The Heavens compatible with any AI generated mod. If you run one alongside Open The Heavens and encounter an error, disable the AI mod and attempt to reproduce the error before reporting a bug.

# Notes/Disclaimer
This is my first mod for Risk of Rain 2 and in general.
I appreciate any constructive advice on implementing the features of this mod, but I would like to treat this as a learning experience and do not want others to effectively backseat the development.
  
If you have any suggestions for mechanics that you feel need changing, feel free to share them, you can find me in the Risk of Rain 2 modding Discord Server as GreatCookieTales.
However, please be aware that I am not going to simply take suggestions and throw them in.
I will view suggestions as a starting point for creating my own ideas, and I may simply not be interested in changing every mechanic suggested to me
As this is meant to be a start for further iteration, I want to preserve the initial ideas that make it in to the mod so that I, other modders, or the development team at Gearbox can continue to learn from them.
  
Once a feature is implemented and released it will not be further changed within the scope of this mod, except to ensure they function correctly where they do not.
The configurability of this mod is in part a method by which individual players can disable and swap out my changes for others' if they so please.

***No GenAI, LLMs, or similar Artificial Intelligence programs were, or will ever be used in the development of this mod***
